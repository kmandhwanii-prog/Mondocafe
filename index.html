the mondo story

Crafted for those who romanticize everyday sips.

We started MONDO as a love letter to slow mornings. In a bustling city, we wanted to carve out a space grounded in warm mocha tones, gentle pink foams, and rich espresso aromas.

We don't do mass-produced syrups or powdered mixes. Our matcha comes directly from Uji, Japan; our whole vanilla beans are hand-scraped into daily creams, and our croissants are pressed hot to order.
